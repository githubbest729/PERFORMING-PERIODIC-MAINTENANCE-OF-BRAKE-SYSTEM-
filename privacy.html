Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This is an independent educational app. It is designed to work without accounts and without collecting personal information.

What is stored on your device

The app stores your study progress, quiz scores, checklist ticks and theme choice in your browser's local storage, and saves the study content in your browser's cache so it works offline. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to any server by the app. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal data collection.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics scripts are included in this version.

Hosting

The app is hosted on a static web hosting service. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app's publisher through the project's repository page.
